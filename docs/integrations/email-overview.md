# Email Intelligence: what it does for you

> A guided tour of Facthory Email Intelligence: connect your Outlook mailbox and turn it into a prioritized, searchable, AI-assisted workspace that drafts replies in your own voice, keeps you on top of what needs an answer, and automates the busywork.

## Email that works for you

Facthory Email Intelligence connects your Microsoft Outlook mailbox and turns it into a calm, prioritized workspace. Instead of scrolling an endless inbox, you see what actually needs a reply, ask plain-language questions about your own threads, draft polished replies in your own writing style, and let dependable rules and automations handle the repetitive work — all in one place, and all under your control.

This overview shows what you can do. The following guides walk each capability step by step.

![Replace: Email Intelligence overview screen](https://placehold.co/1200x675/1a1a2e/eaeaea?text=Replace%3A+Email+Intelligence+overview)

## What you can do

- **See what matters first.** Every message is intelligently prioritized, so the mail that needs your attention today rises to the top — with a clear reason for each.

- **Ask your mailbox anything.** Search by person, topic, date, or attachment, or simply ask a question in plain language and get an answer grounded in your real threads.

- **Reply in your own voice.** Facthory learns your writing style from your sent mail and drafts replies that sound like you — always for your review before anything is sent.

- **Never lose track of a reply.** Reply-urgency bands (Today, This week, No rush) tell you when something is owed an answer and why.

- **Automate the routine.** Create tidy rules that file and organize mail, and set up automations that draft consistent replies at scale — with a human always in the loop.

- **Stay on top of meetings.** Invitations surface with their response state and a one-click link back to the original email.

> Everything is private to you. Your mailbox is only ever visible to you — teammates in the same workspace can never see your email.

## How it fits together

Think of Email Intelligence as four layers that build on each other:

1. **Connect** — a secure, one-time Microsoft sign-in links your mailbox.

1. **Understand** — Facthory quietly organizes and prioritizes your mail in the background.

1. **Assist** — you search, ask, and draft with an assistant that knows your threads and your tone.

1. **Automate** — you promote the patterns you trust into rules and automations.

![Replace: Prioritized inbox with priority icons and reply-urgency](https://placehold.co/1200x675/16213e/eaeaea?text=Replace%3A+Prioritized+inbox)

## Your privacy and control

- You connect your own account with Microsoft's secure sign-in — Facthory never asks for your password.

- You choose whether Facthory can send on your behalf, or only prepare drafts for you to send.

- You can disconnect at any time, which stops syncing and removes stored access.

- **Search and ask your mailbox** — find anything and get grounded answers.

- **Priorities, smart replies and your writing tone** — stay on top of what needs an answer.

- **Rules and automations** — put the routine on autopilot, safely.
